📖 Help & Guide

🎻 About Violin Ear Training v2.7.2

Designed for precision acoustic tracking and violin intonation practice. Listen to your microphone or analyze uploaded video/audio files in real-time.

🔬 Engines: Precision (YIN) for high accuracy, Basic (Auto-Corr) for low latency, or Harmonic (HPS) optimized for violin's rich harmonics.

✨ v2.7.2: Duration slider shifts all following notes; dropdown sorted by finger position (G(0), G(1), G(2)…).

📦 Data Backup

Export all settings, history, and workspace into a .vpack file via Settings → Backup & Restore, and import it on any device.

💾 Practice History

Click "Save" to archive your session. In the history log, click a title to rename it, 📂 to restore, or ✕ to delete.

👆 Interactive Elements

Tap any note in the log or tablature to edit its pitch, duration, volume, and manual breaks. Tap the fingerboard circles to hear pitches.

📊 Scoring: 15¢ Tolerance

Intonation accuracy is evaluated against a 15-cent tolerance:

  • 0¢ = 100% (perfection)
  • ±15¢ = 0% threshold
  • Beyond ±15¢ = negative (sharp/flat outliers)
